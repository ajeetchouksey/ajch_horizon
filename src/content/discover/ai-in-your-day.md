AI isn't just something in labs or in the news — it's already quietly working in a lot of apps and devices you probably use without even thinking about it.

## Where it's hiding

- **Video and music recommendations.** When an app suggests "you might like this next," it's an AI comparing patterns in what you've watched or listened to before against what other people with similar taste enjoyed.
- **Voice assistants.** Asking a smart speaker for the weather involves AI turning your spoken words into text, figuring out what you're actually asking, and picking an answer — several AI steps happening in under a second.
- **Game bots.** The characters that chase you, dodge your attacks, or team up with you in a video game are often following AI-driven decision patterns, not a fixed script for every possible situation.
- **Autocorrect and predictive text.** Your keyboard guessing the next word you're about to type is a small, everyday AI pattern-guesser at work.
- **Photo filters and camera tricks.** Face filters that put animal ears on you, or a camera that automatically finds "the best shot" — both rely on AI recognizing patterns in what a face or a good photo looks like.

## A concrete example

Think about the last time a video app queued up "up next" content and it was weirdly close to what you actually wanted to watch. That wasn't luck. The app's AI compared patterns in your viewing history to patterns from millions of other viewers, found people with similar taste, and guessed you'd probably like what they liked too.

It's the same pattern-guessing idea from "What is AI, really?" — just applied to *your* habits instead of cat photos.

## Why noticing this matters

Once you start noticing AI in your everyday apps, you start understanding *why* they behave the way they do — including the annoying parts. If your recommendations feel stuck showing you the same kind of thing over and over, that's the pattern-guessing working exactly as designed: it's repeating what worked before, not necessarily giving you something genuinely new. Knowing that helps you use these tools more on *your* terms.

> **Quick check:** Pick one app or device you used today. Can you spot a moment where it was probably guessing a pattern about you (what you like, what you meant, what you'd click) instead of just following a fixed rule?
