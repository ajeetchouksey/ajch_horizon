## 3. Our Core Privacy Promise

Spark is designed to be **privacy-first and safe by default**. Our guiding principle is **data minimisation**: we try not to collect personal data at all, especially from children. Wherever a feature *can* work without collecting your information, we build it that way.

---

## 4. What Information We Collect (and What We Don't)

### 4.1 Information we do NOT collect from children
We do **not** ask children to provide, and we do not knowingly collect:
- Names, home addresses, or school names
- Email addresses or phone numbers
- Photos, voice recordings, or videos of children
- Passwords or login credentials (no child accounts required)
- Precise location data

### 4.2 Information collected automatically (technical data)
Like most websites, our servers and hosting provider may automatically process **limited technical data** needed to deliver the site securely, such as:
- **IP address** (used transiently for security and to deliver the page; not used to identify or profile children)
- **Browser type and device type**
- **Pages viewed and time of visit** (aggregated, non-identifying)

We keep this to the minimum necessary to keep the site working, safe, and reliable. We do **not** use it to build profiles of children or to serve advertising.

### 4.3 Information you choose to give us (grown-ups only)
Some optional features — such as **contacting us**, a **newsletter**, or a **parent/teacher account** — may ask an adult to provide an email address. These features are intended for **parents, teachers, and adult users**, and we only use the information for the purpose it was given.

---

## 5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

- Spark does **not** use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.
- We may use a small number of **strictly necessary** cookies or local storage to make the site function (for example, remembering your chosen age path or dark-mode setting). These do not identify you personally.
- If we ever introduce optional analytics, we will use a **privacy-friendly, cookieless** approach and update this policy first.

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

---

## 6. Interactive AI Features (Very Important)

Some parts of Spark let you interact with AI tools (for example, a learning sandbox or a "Prompt Playground"). To keep you safe:

- These features are designed as **safe, sandboxed experiences.**
- **Please never type personal information** (your real name, address, school, passwords, or photos) into any AI tool — on Spark or anywhere else. This is one of our Golden Safety Rules.
- We do **not** use anything children type into learning tools to identify them or to build advertising profiles.
- Where an AI feature relies on a third-party provider, that interaction is limited to delivering the educational feature, and we choose providers with appropriate safeguards.

## 7. How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above only to:
- Deliver, maintain, and secure the Spark website
- Understand, in an aggregated and non-identifying way, which lessons are helpful
- Respond to messages from adults who contact us
- Meet our legal and safety obligations

We do **not** use information to advertise to children, and we do **not** sell personal data — ever.

## 8. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where we process personal data under the EU/UK GDPR, we rely on:
- **Legitimate interests** — to run a secure, functioning website (kept minimal and balanced against your rights).
- **Consent** — for any optional feature (e.g., a newsletter), which an adult can withdraw at any time.
- **Legal obligation** — where the law requires us to process or retain certain data.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Protecting children is central to Spark.

- Spark is built so that children can learn **without providing personal information.**
- Under the EU GDPR, children in Germany are protected up to the age of **16**; under the US COPPA, protections apply to children under **13**. We honour the **stricter** standard.
- We do **not knowingly** collect personal data from a child. If a child has provided personal data to us without appropriate parental/guardian consent, a parent or guardian can contact us and we will **delete it promptly.**
- Any feature that would involve collecting a child's personal data requires **verifiable consent from a parent, guardian, or teacher** first.

**Parents and teachers:** you can contact us at any time to review, correct, or delete information, or to ask questions about your child's use of Spark.

---

## 10. How We Share Information

We do **not sell, rent, or trade** personal information. We only share limited data with:
- **Service providers** who help us run the site (e.g., secure hosting), under contracts that require them to protect the data and use it only for our purposes.
- **Legal authorities**, if strictly required by law.

We never share children's personal data for marketing or advertising.

---

## 11. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for **as long as necessary** for the purpose it was collected, and no longer:
- Technical/server logs: kept for a short period for security, then deleted or anonymised.
- Adult contact/newsletter data: kept until you ask us to delete it or withdraw consent.
